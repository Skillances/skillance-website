## 7. Bookings, pricing modes, and payments

All prices on the Platform are displayed in South African Rand (ZAR) and, where applicable, include VAT. Payments are processed through our third-party payment provider (currently WalletDoc). Skillance does not store full card details.

Bookings follow one of two pricing modes:

- **Hourly bookings.** The total is derived from the Freelancer's hourly rate and the session duration. The customer pays the full amount up front; funds are held in escrow with our payment provider until the session is completed and the dispute window has closed.
- **Invoice bookings.** The customer pays a **non-refundable connection fee of R50** which unlocks in-app chat with the Freelancer. The Freelancer then issues an in-app invoice; the customer may accept or decline. If accepted and paid, the invoice total is held in escrow. If declined, the connection fee is not refunded.

Sessions are supported by a **session PIN** to evidence attendance and release escrow correctly. After session completion, a **24-hour dispute window** applies before undisputed payouts are released. Scheduled auto-completion and payout processing are handled by our backend.

## 8. Platform commission and fees

Skillance earns revenue as a **commission-based marketplace**. Unless a different rate is agreed in writing:

- **Booking commission:** **8–9% including VAT** on the booking total for completed bookings, deducted before payout to the Freelancer.
- **Connection fee:** a non-refundable R50 fee on the invoice path.
- **Payment processing fees** may be absorbed by Skillance or allocated as disclosed in the Platform from time to time.

Fee amounts and rates are displayed in-app before checkout and may change on notice. Historical commission on a completed booking is finalised based on the rate applied at the time of completion.
