# Skillance Service Categories

> Plain-text catalogue of every service category bookable on Skillance in South Africa. Each category is served by ID-verified local freelancers who accept bookings, pricing, and payment through the app.

Canonical page: https://skillance.co.za/services

---

## How Skillance works

1. Browse a category below or search the Skillance app by keyword or location.
2. Open a freelancer's profile to see their rates, availability, portfolio, and public ratings.
3. Send a booking request with a date, time, and scope description. The freelancer accepts or declines.
4. After acceptance, chat opens for final details. Payment is held in escrow by our licensed South African payment partner.
5. After the booking is marked complete and the short dispute window closes, the freelancer is paid (less the 8–9% VAT-inclusive platform fee).

Customers never pay off-platform. Off-app payments void Skillance's refund and dispute protections.

## Categories

### Handyman
Electricians, plumbers, carpenters, painters, tilers, roofers, and general maintenance pros for urgent repairs and polished finishing work. Freelancers are chosen through public ratings and reviews — no certification gate.
- Typical subcategories: Electrician, Plumber, Carpenter, Painter, Tiler, Roofer.
- Best for: emergency fixes, home improvements, property-management maintenance.

### Education & tutoring
Private tutors, test-prep coaches, language teachers, and online course mentors for primary, secondary, university, and adult learners.
- Typical subcategories: School subjects, test prep (Matric, IEB, university), language learning, online courses.

### Cleaning
House cleaning, deep cleaning, office cleaning, post-construction cleaning, and carpet cleaning from reliable local cleaners.
- Typical subcategories: House Cleaning, Deep Cleaning, Office Cleaning, Carpet Cleaning.
- Best for: weekly/fortnightly household cleans, move-in/move-out deep cleans, offices.

### Pet care
Dog walkers, pet sitters, groomers, and trainers who keep pets happy and healthy while owners work or travel.
- Typical subcategories: Dog Walking, Pet Sitting, Pet Grooming, Pet Training.

### Fitness & wellness
Personal trainers, yoga instructors, nutritionists, and massage therapists for at-home, at-gym, or remote coaching.
- Typical subcategories: Personal Trainers, Wellness, Nutritionist, Massage Therapist.

### Automotive
Mobile mechanics, car detailing, tire service, battery service, and vehicle diagnostics that come to you.
- Typical subcategories: Mobile Mechanic, Car Detailing, Tire Service, Battery Service.

### Gardening & landscaping
Lawn care, tree felling, landscaping, and garden maintenance for homes, estates, and complexes.

### Technology & IT
Device setup, home/office network support, PC repair, and general software help for non-technical users and small businesses.

### Professional services
Accountants, bookkeepers, legal consultants, and business advisors for freelancers, SMEs, and households.

### Personal services
Mobile hair, beauty, nails, and makeup artists — including event-day services and spa-at-home bookings.

### Photography
Event, wedding, portrait, product, and real-estate photographers.

### Videography
Wedding videographers, event recording, social-media content creators, and promotional video specialists.

### Music
Musicians, DJs, vocalists, and instrument tutors — bookable for events, lessons, or recordings.

### Influencing & content
Social-media influencers and content creators available for brand collaborations and campaign partnerships.

## Trust model

- Every freelancer passes South African ID verification and profile-photo matching before they can take bookings.
- Skillance does not require trade certifications or qualifications to register — quality is surfaced through public ratings and reviews on every completed booking.
- Payments are held in escrow until the customer confirms completion (or the grace period passes).
- Disputes go through an in-app flow with escrow frozen until resolved. Outcomes follow the Consumer Protection Act and Skillance's published Terms.
