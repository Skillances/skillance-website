## 1. Purpose and who this applies to

This Cookie Policy explains how Skillance ("we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on skillance.co.za and related web properties we operate (the "Site"). It should be read together with our Privacy Policy (https://skillance.co.za/privacy-policy).

Where cookies or similar technologies involve personal information, we process that information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 ("POPIA", Act 4 of 2013) and other applicable South African law. This policy is intended to meet POPIA's transparency expectations (including section 18-style notification where personal information is collected through the Site).

This page is for transparency and operational clarity. It does not constitute legal advice.

## 2. Responsible party

The operator responsible for this Site is Skillance (RIMITSO MANAGEMENT SERVICES (PTY) LTD). For privacy and cookie-related questions, contact services@skillance.co.za.

## 3. What we mean by cookies and similar technologies

"Cookies" are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. We also use closely related technologies that store or read information on your device, such as **local storage** (for example, to remember your cookie choices on this device) and **pixels or tags** loaded through our tag manager where you have granted consent.

## 4. How we group cookies and why we use them

We group technologies into three categories, matching the choices in our cookie banner ("Accept all", "Reject non-essential", and "Customize").

### 4.1 Strictly necessary (always on)

Needed for core Site functions such as security, load balancing, session continuity, fraud prevention, and remembering your cookie preference itself. Used based on our legitimate interests in operating a secure, reliable service and, where applicable, to perform our contract with you when you use account features.

Examples include first-party session or authentication-related storage, security-related cookies, and the local storage entry we use to store your consent state.

### 4.2 Analytics (optional)

If you opt in, we use analytics technologies to understand aggregate traffic, performance, and how the Site is used. We use **Google Tag Manager** and related Google measurement tools, configured to respect **Google Consent Mode** defaults and updates based on your choice. Analytics storage remains denied until you grant consent for analytics.

### 4.3 Marketing / advertising (optional)

If you opt in, marketing technologies may be used for advertising, remarketing, campaign measurement, or similar purposes. Ad-related storage (ad storage and ad personalization signals in Consent Mode) remains denied until you grant consent for marketing.

## 5. Consent, withdrawal, and changing your mind

Under POPIA, consent must be voluntary, specific, and informed where we rely on consent for non-essential processing. When you first visit (or if no valid choice is stored), we present a banner so you can accept all, reject non-essential, or customize analytics and marketing separately.

You may **withdraw or change consent** at any time where processing is based on consent. Clear Site data for Skillance in your browser (which removes the locally stored consent record and causes the banner to re-appear), or contact services@skillance.co.za for assistance. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing that occurred before withdrawal.

You can also use your browser's cookie controls and, where available, industry opt-out tools. Third-party tools may still read their own cookies subject to their policies and your settings.

## 6. Record of your choice (server-side)

When you save a choice, we may create a **security and compliance record** in our systems (audit log entry) containing the policy version, decision type, analytics and marketing toggles, a timestamp, and technical metadata such as IP address and browser user-agent string. If you are logged in, the record may be associated with your user account. This supports accountability, dispute handling, and regulatory expectations.

## 7. Retention

Strictly necessary cookies and local storage used to operate the Site are kept only as long as needed for their purpose. Analytics and marketing data held by third parties is retained according to their retention settings and your choices. Our own compliance records are retained for a limited period consistent with our Privacy Policy.

## 8. Cross-border processing

Some optional technologies (for example, Google services) may involve processing in countries outside South Africa. Where personal information is transferred across borders, we take POPIA into account (including Chapter 9 requirements where applicable) and rely on appropriate mechanisms offered by our providers.

## 9. Your rights under POPIA

You may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing, to object on legitimate grounds, and to withdraw consent where processing is consent-based. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa) at https://inforegulator.org.za/.

For details and how to exercise rights, see our Privacy Policy and contact services@skillance.co.za.

## 10. Children

Our services are not directed at children in a way that requires special cookie processing beyond our general approach. Contact us if you have concerns.

## 11. Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date changes and we may bump the policy version used in consent records.
