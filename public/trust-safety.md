# Trust & Safety on Skillance

## Verification

- Before a provider can take bookings, we check their identity document and match it to their profile photo. This confirms who they are. It doesn't assess their skills.
- **No certification gate.** Skillance does not check trade qualifications or references. For work that legally needs a certificate (such as an electrical COC), confirm this with the provider before you book.
- **Profile-photo check.** The photo you see on a provider's profile is matched against their identity document.

## Payments

Payments aren't live yet. Details will be published before bookings open.

## Session PIN

The customer shows a session PIN at the start of the session, and the provider submits it to confirm the session has begun. The PIN does not mark the job as complete.

## After the session

After the session is marked complete, a **24-hour dispute window** starts.

## Personal information

- See our Privacy Policy for how we handle personal information: https://skillance.co.za/privacy-policy
- Customers can request access, correction, or deletion of their data by emailing services@skillance.co.za or using Settings → Delete account.

## Reporting and disputes

- Report a booking or user from the in-app menu on the booking screen, or email services@skillance.co.za with the booking ID.
- Serious reports (harassment, fraud, safety risks) are escalated to Skillance Trust & Safety.
- Disputes: our team reviews the chat, booking timeline and both sides' submissions. Outcomes follow our Terms.
- For suspected fraud, email fraud@skillance.co.za with screenshots and we'll investigate.

## Our commitment

- **ID check.** Identity document matched to the profile photo. It confirms who the provider is. It doesn't assess their skills.
- **Your information.** See our Privacy Policy.
- **Reviews from customers.** We moderate reviews under our Terms.
- **Human support.** Email support@skillance.co.za. Support hours are Mon-Fri 09:00-17:00.
