## Verification

- **South African ID check.** Every freelancer must submit a South African ID or valid work permit. The ID number is validated and the document photo is matched against the freelancer's profile photo before they can take bookings. A verified badge appears on profiles once this is complete.
- **No certification gate.** Skillance does not require trade certifications or qualifications to register. Anyone eligible to work in South Africa can join. Customers judge quality from public ratings and reviews left after real bookings.
- **Optional trust signals.** Freelancers may optionally upload a police clearance certificate to display an extra trust badge on their profile. This is never required to register or to take bookings.
- **Profile-photo check.** The photo you see on a freelancer's profile is matched against the ID to make sure the person you're booking is who they say they are.

## Payments and escrow

- **Escrow first.** When a customer confirms a booking, the payment is held by our licensed South African payment partner (TBC). Freelancers only receive their share after the customer confirms the job is complete (or the short dispute window closes).
- **Card and EFT only.** Payments are processed through regulated channels — card (Visa/Mastercard) and instant EFT. Skillance never stores full card numbers.
- **No cash in-app.** Off-app payments void Skillance protections and are against our Terms.
- **VAT and transparency.** All amounts shown are VAT-inclusive where applicable, and the service fee (8–9% including VAT) is itemised before checkout.

## Session PIN and completion

- Bookings use a **session PIN** model that confirms the freelancer actually attended. The customer shares the PIN at the start of the session; the PIN entry is recorded.
- After the session is marked complete, a **24-hour dispute window** starts. During the window, escrow stays frozen. After the window closes without a dispute, the freelancer's payout is released automatically.
- If the PIN was never entered, escrow is held pending review, regardless of the window.

## Data protection (POPIA)

- Skillance is POPIA-compliant. We only collect personal information we need to run the marketplace, we store it securely, and we **never sell it**.
- Sensitive data (ID documents, selfies) is treated as "special" personal information under POPIA and is handled with stricter controls.
- Customers can request access, correction, or deletion of their data at any time by emailing services@skillance.co.za or using Settings → Delete account.
- Full privacy terms: https://skillance.co.za/privacy-policy

## Reporting and disputes

- Report a booking or user from the in-app menu on the booking screen, or email services@skillance.co.za with the booking ID.
- Serious reports (harassment, fraud, safety risks) are escalated to Skillance Trust & Safety immediately.
- Disputes open a review of chat logs, PIN evidence, payment history, and any submitted evidence. The funds stay in escrow while the team investigates.
- Outcomes follow our Terms and South African consumer law (CPA, ECTA).
- For suspected fraud, email fraud@skillance.co.za with screenshots — we can freeze escrow during investigation.
