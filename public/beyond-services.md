## 05 Website redesigns for small businesses

Fast, credible marketing sites for South African trades and local services.

Redesigns for plumbers, dentists, guest houses, electricians, self-catering stays, and similar businesses. Mobile-first sites with clear calls to action, WhatsApp and enquiry paths, hosting on Cloudflare Pages, optional forms, cookieless analytics, and email setup (Google Workspace or Zoho). POPIA operator agreement for visitor data on client sites.

**Industry-aware content:** dental and medical sites follow HPCSA advertising rules; plumbing sites show PIRB registration only when registered; electrical sites show DoEL contractor registration when applicable; guest-house sites show TGCSA grading while current. Sites include POPIA privacy notice, ECT Act disclosures, and a link to your PAIA manual.
