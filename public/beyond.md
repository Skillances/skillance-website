## What Skillance Beyond builds

1. **Website redesigns** for South African small businesses (trades, guest houses, dental practices, electricians, self-catering, and similar): mobile-first, clear calls to action, WhatsApp and enquiry paths.
2. **Custom software**: portals, internal tools, workflows, and integrations.
3. **SaaS and web apps**: browser products with accounts, billing, and production cloud infrastructure.
4. **Mobile apps** (iOS and Android) when the product needs them; Skillance ships its own marketplace apps in Flutter.
5. **Integrations**: APIs, payments, sign-in, messaging, and cloud hosting (details in each project proposal).

Extended catalogue: https://skillance.co.za/beyond-services.md

## How projects start (websites)

1. **Free demo** of your own site concept (no fee, no obligation).
2. **Fixed quote in writing** with scope and VAT-inclusive total (website tiers use fixed pricing after the demo; larger software uses a separate proposal).
3. **Build** with structured revision rounds; you approve before launch.
4. **Launch** on modern hosting (Cloudflare Pages for marketing sites; R0 monthly hosting from Skillance for standard client sites).
5. **Optional care plans** after launch, or you keep the code and run it yourself.

Full process: https://skillance.co.za/beyond-approach.md
